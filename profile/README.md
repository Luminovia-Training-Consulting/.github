# Luminovia Training & Consulting

**Understand technology. Put it to work.**

Practical training, learning design and consulting for businesses, education providers and universities. Founded and led by [Carina Sophie Schoppe](https://carinaschoppe.com/), based in Brisbane, Australia, with live online delivery in **English and German**.

**[Explore training](https://luminovia.org/training)** · **[Discuss a project](https://luminovia.org/contact)** · [Consulting](https://luminovia.org/consulting)

## What we do

- **Training and workshops.** Clear explanations, practical labs and guided exercises, from short briefings to multi-week courses.
- **AI and IT consulting.** Assess use cases, review tools and processes, and plan practical steps for AI adoption, automation and digital projects.
- **Learning design.** Develop curricula, learning paths, course materials, labs and assessments for adult and professional education.
- **Talks and guest lectures.** Make AI, cybersecurity and digital transformation accessible to technical and business audiences.

[Training and programme formats](https://luminovia.org/offers) · [Client references](https://luminovia.org/references)

## Areas we teach and work in

| Area | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| **AI and responsible use** | AI literacy, generative AI, prompting, agentic workflows, output evaluation and governance |
| **Software and data** | Python, Java, C#, Kotlin, SQL, databases, APIs, automation and data analysis |
| **IT and cybersecurity** | Networking, Linux, cloud concepts, IT service management and security awareness |
| **Business and digital work** | Business administration, accounting fundamentals, agile methods, process improvement and digital transformation |

Courses combine concepts with tasks learners can practise, review and apply at work. Content and exercises are adapted to the audience and agreed learning goals.
